Got questions? We’ve got answers.

Everything you need to know about the offer.

How do I qualify for your service?

You need to have a transactional/ecommerce website that has a turnover of £1million (GPB) or equivalent.

Is it really free?

Yes. To a point. Our service is completely free up until our conversion rate endeavours have achieved the conversion rate target. In turn this should have yielded more revenue from your site which ordinarily would more than cover our contracted fee.

When do I have to pay anything?

After we’ve hit the mutually agreed conversion rate target, you are then contracted to pay us a pre-agreed monthly fee, by Direct Debit, for a period of 12 months. If we don’t hit the conversion rate target - then you don’t pay us anything.

What happens if you don’t hit the conversion rate target?

If we are unable to hit the agreed conversion rate target you don’t pay us anything.

How long will the free period of work last?

We’d like to think this will be a matter of a few weeks - but might be longer - either way you won’t pay us anything until we achieve our target.

Do I have to sign a contract?

Yes. This is to protect us against the free time spent in our endeavours to hit the conversion rate target. We will ask you to sign our contract before we start any work.

Can I cancel my contract any time?

You can cancel the contract within 14 days of signing. Following that, you can only cancel by giving us 30 days notice prior to the end of the contract.

What happens after the contract expires?

You can either walk away or we continue to work together, we’ll discuss the options and hopefully agree on a new contract so that we can continue to do great things together.

Once you achieve what you say you’re going to achieve does the service stop?

No. We continue to further improve your conversion rate beyond the agreed conversion rate target for the term of the contract - setting a new target to aim for.

What work do you do after you’ve hit the conversion rate target?

For the 12 months we perform a minimum of one conversion test per month.

If a test is successful do Uplyft perform the work required to my website?

No. We simply provide the information for any successful test to yourself (or your development team) and ask that it be rolled out to your website.
